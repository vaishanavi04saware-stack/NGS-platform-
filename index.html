Talk track

Research use only. AXELIOS 1 is not for use in diagnostic procedures. Values tagged Spec come from Roche's published AXELIOS 1 spec sheet. Values tagged Assumption describe a typical current high-output workflow and should be replaced with the customer's numbers. Cost per sample is not modeled. The average sample is assumed to wait half the time it takes a batch to fill.

Sources
  1. AXELIOS 1 spec sheet (Roche): tunable runs of about 15 minutes to 4 hours; about 16 genomes at 30x per 4-hour run; about 5.5 hours per run; up to 64 genomes a day; prep about 2 to 13 hours; sensor reusable up to 20 runs within 90 days; on-instrument basecalling and alignment in parallel with sequencing.
  2. Roche AXELIOS 1 launch release (June 29, 2026): same-day whole-genome sequencing; small and large projects on one instrument; research use only.
  3. Lab Manager on core-lab trade-offs: cores choose between running a partly filled flow cell and waiting to fill it.
  4. UC Davis Genome Center FAQ: high-output data about 2 to 5 weeks after library submission, varying with the queue.
